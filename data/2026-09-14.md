### [1] [Remarks on invertible phases with non-onsite symmetry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12042)
*Ryohei Kobayashi,Kansei Inamura,Ken Shiozaki*

Main category: cond-mat.str-el

TL;DR: 本文研究由非格点对称性保护的不可逆相，特别是携带非平凡格点异常指标的对称性。通过构造p+ip超导体上精确的指数准局域Z₄^F对称性，证明异常格点对称性可以允许非SRE的不可逆相，并将对称兼容的不可逆相集合从c₋∈Z移动至c₋∈Z+1/2。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统观点认为格点异常是通向对称短程纠缠（SRE）态的障碍。作者希望探究：携带非平凡格点异常指标的非格点对称性是否仍能允许不可逆相存在，以及这类相如何改变对称兼容的不可逆相集合。这一问题的动机源于对格点异常与连续统't Hooft异常之间差异的深入理解。

Method: 作者在(2+1)维考虑费米子Z₄^F对称性。对于格点（onsite）Z₄^F对称性，对称不可逆相具有整数手征中心电荷c₋∈Z；而具有非平凡格点异常指标的非格点对称性则阻碍所有这些相，尽管其连续统't Hooft异常平凡。作者通过构造p+ip超导体（c₋=1/2）的精确指数准局域Z₄^F对称性来解决这一矛盾，计算其格点异常，并通过对费米子宇称进行规范化得到由Z₄对称性富化的Ising拓扑序，用融合2-范畴描述其对称结构。此外还展示了p+ip态允许扩大的非格点U(1)^f对称性。

Result: 1) 构造的p+ip超导体Z₄^F对称性确实禁止c₋∈Z的不可逆态，允许的不可逆相被移动至c₋∈Z+1/2；2) 规范化费米子宇称后得到由Z₄对称性富化的Ising拓扑序，其对称结构可用融合2-范畴描述；3) p+ip态承认扩大的非格点U(1)^f对称性，提供了不可逆相中分数量子霍尔响应的类比；4) 该非格点U(1)^f对称性同样禁止携带c₋∈Z的陈绝缘体，但与c₋∈Z+1/2的不可逆态兼容。

Conclusion: 非格点对称性可以允许非SRE的不可逆相，并改变对称兼容的不可逆相集合。格点异常与连续统't Hooft异常的差异导致不可逆相从整数手征中心电荷偏移至半整数。这一结果激发了对由非格点对称性富化的新型不可逆相和自旋液体的系统性研究。

Abstract: We study invertible phases protected by non-onsite symmetries carrying nontrivial lattice anomaly indices. While lattice anomalies are usually viewed as obstructions to symmetric short-range-entangled (SRE) states, we show that anomalous lattice symmetries can nevertheless admit invertible phases that are not SRE, and can moreover shift the set of symmetry-compatible invertible phases. In particular, we consider a fermionic $\mathbb Z_4^F$ symmetry in (2+1) dimensions. For an onsite $\mathbb Z_4^F$ symmetry, symmetric invertible phases have integer chiral central charge $c_-\in\mathbb Z$, whereas a non-onsite symmetry with nontrivial lattice anomaly index obstructs all such phases despite having trivial continuum 't Hooft anomaly. We resolve this by constructing an exact exponentially quasi-local $\mathbb Z_4^F$ symmetry of a $p+ip$ superconductor with $c_-=1/2$. We compute its lattice anomaly, and find that the symmetry indeed forbids $c_-\in \mathbb{Z}$ invertible states. The allowed invertible phases are consequently shifted to $c_-\in\mathbb Z+1/2$. By gauging the fermion parity of the $p+ip$ superconductor, one obtains an Ising topological order enriched by a $\mathbb Z_4$ symmetry. We describe the corresponding symmetry structure in terms of a fusion 2-category. We further show that the $p+ip$ state admits an enlarged non-onsite $U(1)^f$ symmetry, providing an analogue of fractional quantum Hall response in an invertible phase. This non-onsite $U(1)^f$ symmetry again forbids Chern insulators carrying $c_-\in\mathbb{Z}$, while being compatible with invertible states with $c_-\in\mathbb{Z}+1/2$. This result motivates a systematic study of new classes of invertible phases and spin liquids enriched by non-onsite symmetries.

### [2] [Spin splitting without symmetry: a nearly compensated ferrimagnet and the origin of altermagnetism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12931)
*Joo Yull Rhee*

Main category: cond-mat.str-el

TL;DR: 该文论证共线交替磁体的自旋劈裂源于磁性轨道及其配体的各向异性排布，而非晶体对称性；对称性只起组织作用（反向或禁戒既有劈裂），不能凭空产生劈裂。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统观点将共线交替磁体的自旋劈裂归因于关联相反自旋子晶格的晶体对称性（旋转或镜面，而非常规反铁磁的平移或反演）。作者要澄清：劈裂的真正起源是磁性轨道及其配体的各向异性排布，这一排布同时决定了对称性本身。

Method: 以三斜（P1）Mn氧化物这一完全补偿亚铁磁体（两个不等价子晶格之间无任何对称性关联）为对象，证明其在整个布里渊区存在自旋劈裂；再将相同配体逐一重新定位以恢复联系两金属位的反演中心，对比劈裂变化。同时引入按操作分辨的对称性层级，并把晶体学点群作为一个连续轴来解读，追踪正交、单斜、三斜各类中强制节点面的数量变化。

Result: 恢复反演中心后，劈裂塌缩为原本约百分之一的残余量，表明劈裂来自排布而非对称性；形式化分析表明对称性只能反向或禁戒已存在的劈裂，无法在排布已使子晶格电子等价时或在轨道本身无各向异性时创造劈裂。强制节点面随正交→单斜→三斜从两个减到一个再到零，而排布产生的劈裂一直保持到最低对称性的三斜端。

Conclusion: 交替磁体与完全补偿亚铁磁体是同一轴的两端：同一种劈裂，一端由对称性组织、另一端未被组织。寻找补偿型自旋劈裂磁体，本质上是在搜索轨道排布而非对称性标签。

Abstract: The spin splitting of collinear altermagnets is usually attributed to the crystal symmetry relating the opposite-spin sublattices --- a rotation or mirror in place of the translation or inversion of a conventional antiferromagnet. We show that this symmetry organizes the splitting but does not produce it; its origin is the anisotropic arrangement of the magnetic orbitals and their ligands, which also fixes the symmetry itself. Two results establish this. A triclinic ($P1$) \ce{Mn} oxide, a fully compensated ferrimagnet whose two inequivalent sublattices are related by no symmetry, is spin split throughout the Brillouin zone; repositioning the same ligands, atom for atom, to restore an inversion centre relating the two metal sites collapses the splitting to a residual two orders of magnitude smaller, so the splitting follows from the arrangement rather than the symmetry. Symmetry, we show formally, can only ever reverse or forbid a splitting that already exists: it cannot create one between sublattices an arrangement has left electronically identical, nor where the underlying orbitals carry no anisotropy to arrange in the first place. Organizing the analysis through an operation-resolved symmetry hierarchy and reading the crystal classes as a single axis, the enforced nodal planes fall from two to one to zero across the orthorhombic, monoclinic, and triclinic classes while the arrangement-generated splitting persists to the base. The altermagnet and the fully compensated ferrimagnet are thus the two ends of one axis --- the same splitting, organized by symmetry at one end and unorganized at the other --- and the search for compensated spin-split magnets is a search over orbital arrangements rather than symmetry labels.

### [3] [Observation of topological surface phonons in diamond with nonlinear optics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12362)
*Qi Wang,Xinyi Liu,Xinyue Sheng,Zhi-Kang Lin,Xiaowei Lu,Xiaosheng Yang,Peining Li,Yizhou Liu,Chang-Hui Li,Wei-Tao Liu,Jian-Hua Jiang*

Main category: cond-mat.mtrl-sci

TL;DR: 本文利用和频光谱首次在金刚石中发现拓扑表面声子，确立金刚石为声子拓扑半金属，并揭示表面无序对拓扑表面声子的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 拓扑量子态在电子体系中已深刻改变对物相的认识，理论预测了固体中的拓扑声子，但缺乏对拓扑表面声子（拓扑声子态的标志性特征）的可信实验验证，该范式尚未确立。

Method: 采用和频光谱（一种可高灵敏探测表面声子的非线性光学光谱技术）对金刚石表面声子进行探测，并化学修饰其表面以引入无序；结合理论计算与实验进行对照分析。

Result: 发现金刚石为声子拓扑半金属，其体态存在拓扑节点线和节点三重简并点；理论与实验一致地揭示了金刚石(111)和(100)表面的拓扑表面声子；通过化学修饰表面，观察到无序对拓扑表面声子的影响。

Conclusion: 该工作为量子拓扑与晶格动力学两大基础领域的桥梁构建奠定基础，并对金刚石基器件和功能界面具有重要启示，同时首次提供了拓扑表面声子的实验证据。

Abstract: Topological quantum states in electronic systems have profoundly transformed the understanding of phases of matter. Recent theories predict novel vibrational topological quantum states, i.e., topological phonons in various solids. However, this paradigm is yet to be established due to the lack of convincing experimental verification of topological surface phonons---a hallmark signature of topological phonon states. Here, we report the discovery of topological surface phonons in diamond using sum-frequency spectroscopy---a nonlinear optical spectroscopy capable of probing surface phonons with high sensitivity. Diamond, known for exceptional hardness and thermal transport, is unveiled as a phonon topological semimetal hosting topological nodal-lines and nexus triple points in the bulk. With consistent theory and experiments, we uncover the resultant topological surface phonons on diamond (111) and (100) surfaces. Moreover, by chemically modifying these surfaces, we reveal the disorder effect on topological surface phonons. These findings pave the way for bridging two fundamental domains: quantum topology and lattice dynamics, besides having important implications on diamond-based devices and functional interfaces.
