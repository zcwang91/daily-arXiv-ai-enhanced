Motivation: 锗是半导体自旋量子比特和自旋电子学的重要平台，但其电子和空穴自旋动力学仍主要依赖唯象模型理解，缺乏从微观机理层面的第一性原理刻画，限制了对自旋弛豫机制的认识与器件设计优化。

Method: 采用杂化泛函能带结构结合全相对论电子-声子相互作用的第一性原理方法，从头计算体相Ge的电子输运和自旋弛豫性质；通过按能谷和声子模式分解自旋翻转散射，识别控制自旋弛豫的微观机制；并研究压应变对空穴自旋寿命的影响机制。

Result: 在无经验参数下，预测的载流子迁移率、速度-场曲线以及电子和空穴自旋弛豫时间在100-400 K范围内与实验定量吻合；揭示了自旋弛豫与动量弛豫虽均由e-ph散射介导，但受不同过程控制；5%压缩双轴应变通过价带劈裂和自旋混合抑制，将空穴自旋寿命提升最高两个数量级。

Conclusion: 该工作提供了Ge中自旋弛豫的完整微观物理图像，确立了应变工程作为延长Ge空穴自旋寿命的实用路径，对Ge-on-Si器件中长寿命空穴自旋的量子技术应用具有重要意义。

Abstract: Germanium is a leading platform for semiconductor spin qubits and spintronics. Yet its electron and hole spin dynamics remain understood primarily through phenomenological models. Here, we predict electronic transport and spin relaxation in bulk Ge entirely from first principles, combining hybrid-functional band structures with fully relativistic electron-phonon ($e$-ph) interactions. Without empirical parameters, we predict carrier mobilities, velocity-field curves, and electron and hole spin relaxation times in close agreement with experiments over 100$-$400 K. By resolving spin-flip scattering by valley and phonon mode, we identify the microscopic mechanisms governing spin relaxation and show that spin and momentum relaxation, although both mediated by $e$-ph scattering, are controlled by distinct processes. We further show that compressive biaxial strain enhances the hole spin lifetime by up to two orders of magnitude at 5% strain, through strain-induced valence-band splitting and suppressed spin mixing. This mechanism is directly relevant to Ge-on-Si devices, where strain provides a practical route to engineering long-lived hole spins for quantum technologies.
